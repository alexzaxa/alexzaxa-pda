What it does

Dine-in ordering

Waiters take orders from any phone or tablet on the restaurant Wi-Fi, split bills, and manage tables in real time.

Phone & delivery orders

A dedicated register desk handles phone-in orders, delivery zones, and customer history.

Kitchen printing

Orders route straight to the kitchen printer, with Greek-language and receipt support built in.

Self-updating

Every install checks for new releases automatically and installs them with one manager click — no technician visit required.
